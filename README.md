# Indonesia Land Watch

Static dashboard of palm oil, pulp & paper and coal companies holding land across Indonesia (Kalimantan, Sumatra, Papua, Sulawesi, Java, Nusa Tenggara, Maluku): owners, listings, revenue, mapped licence boundaries, NASA FIRMS fire detections and modelled haze.

Data snapshot: fires 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-14, compiled 2026-10-14. Company figures marked as estimates should be checked against filings. Outside Kalimantan and Sumatra, mapped licence coverage is thin; many Papua and Sulawesi holdings are shown as approximate subsidiary locations rather than boundaries. A fire detection inside a licence shows where a fire occurred, not who caused it.

Sources: Global Forest Watch (oil palm and wood-fibre concessions), ESDM Geoportal (coal licences), NASA FIRMS and GIBS, CAMS via Open-Meteo, geoBoundaries, company filings and press/NGO reports (see the company profiles).
