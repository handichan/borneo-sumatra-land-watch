# Borneo–Sumatra Land Watch

Static dashboard of palm oil, pulp & paper and coal companies holding land in Kalimantan and Sumatra (Indonesia): owners, listings, revenue, mapped licence boundaries, NASA FIRMS fire detections and modelled haze.

Data snapshot: fires 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-13, compiled 2026-10-14. Company figures marked as estimates should be checked against filings. A fire detection inside a licence shows where a fire occurred, not who caused it.

Sources: Global Forest Watch (oil palm and wood-fibre concessions), ESDM Geoportal (coal licences), NASA FIRMS and GIBS, CAMS via Open-Meteo, geoBoundaries, company filings and press/NGO reports (see the company profiles).
