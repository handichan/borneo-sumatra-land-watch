# Indonesia Land Watch

Static dashboard of companies holding land concessions across Indonesia — palm oil, pulp & paper, other agriculture & plantations, natural-forest logging, coal, metal mining and quarrying/cement — in (Kalimantan, Sumatra, Papua, Sulawesi, Java, Nusa Tenggara, Maluku): owners, listings, revenue, mapped licence boundaries, forest-area use permits (PPKH), tree cover loss, NASA FIRMS fire detections and modelled haze.

Data snapshot: fires 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-14, compiled 2026-10-14. Company figures marked as estimates should be checked against filings. Agriculture companies (sugar, rubber, food estates) have no national boundary dataset and appear as approximate points. Outside Kalimantan and Sumatra, mapped licence coverage is thin; many Papua and Sulawesi holdings are shown as approximate subsidiary locations rather than boundaries. A fire detection inside a licence shows where a fire occurred, not who caused it.

The Overview, Conglomerates and Fires pages include cards from WALHI's October 2026 fire-actor report ("Siapa di Balik Api?"), with a cross-check of the companies it names against this dataset.

A Forest change page shows real satellite mosaics (Landsat GLS 2000–2010, Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016–2025) of Sumatra, Borneo, Java, Sulawesi and Papua with a year slider and a land-use layer, on by default, for the selected year: natural forest (Hansen/UMD tree cover limited to SBTN natural forest 2020 and primary forest 2001), oil palm and pulpwood (GFW planted trees v2 with oil palm and pulpwood licences), cities and structures (Esri / Impact Observatory 10 m land cover) and mines (Maus et al. 2022). Zoomed out it uses pre-rendered tiles that keep each class's true share. A blind check of 113 points against satellite imagery agreed at 78% (forest 28/31, mines 9/11, oil palm 14/18).

Sources: WALHI Eksekutif Nasional (2026), Global Forest Watch (oil palm and wood-fibre concessions), Global Forest Watch logging concessions, ESDM Geoportal (mining and quarry licences, PPKH), Hansen/UMD tree cover loss, NASA FIRMS and GIBS, CAMS via Open-Meteo, geoBoundaries, USGS/NASA Global Land Survey via Esri, Sentinel-2 cloudless by EOX (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), GFW tree cover statistics, company filings and press/NGO reports (see the company profiles).
